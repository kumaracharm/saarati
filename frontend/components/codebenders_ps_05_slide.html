Claim What’s Yours — SAARTHI

Plain-Language Eligibility, Document Readiness & Guided Claims

Problem & Requirements Brief

Transparent Rule Reasoning · Zero Sensitive ID Storage

01 · The Problem

High Unclaimed Gap

Hundreds of central & state schemes—scholarships, pensions, health cover, housing, and farm support—go unclaimed because:

  • Notices & portals are scattered with dense, hard-to-read eligibility rules.
  • Required paperwork is unclear, causing missed windows & costly middlemen.

Who It Serves (State-Focused)

High-miss groups in a specific state context:

Low-Income Students Senior Citizens Small Farmers Women Entrepreneurs Daily-Wage Families

02 · Challenge & Solution Pillars

5 Core Requirements

Describe a situation in plain language—get likely fits, exact reasons, documents, and next steps:

  1. 1. Plain-Language Input: Typed or spoken (incl. regional language) instead of a rigid long form.
  2. 2. Explainable Matching: Shows which schemes fit and which rule each match relies on.
  3. 3. Document Checklist & Guide: Personalised docs plus step-by-step apply & follow-up steps.
  4. 4. Deadlines & Tracking: Reminders and application status tracking so claims don’t lapse.
  5. 5. Verified Official Sources: Official portal links paired with last-verified dates.

03 · Guardrails

Safe & Auditable
  • Says “likely eligible”—never “guaranteed”.
  • No Aadhaar or sensitive ID numbers collected.
  • Public scheme data & editable rules; sample profiles only.

Post-Demo Q&A Ready

Prepared answers ready right after the live demo for: (1) which details matter, (2) rule accuracy as rules change, and (3) who pays (NGOs, CSR, banks, CSCs, state govt).
